A sneaker store, built to learn.

Outsole is a demonstration store made for a university midterm project. It shows how a real shop works from first click to printed invoice, using only a front end.

What you can try

  • Search, filter and sort ten sample sneakers
  • Pick a size and colour, then add to cart
  • Check out with a validated form and demo payment choices
  • Open and print an A4 HTML invoice

How it is built

  • Angular with server-side rendering and hybrid rendering
  • Home and About are prerendered; Shop and product pages render on the server
  • Cart, checkout and invoices run in your browser and use local storage
  • Bootstrap 5, SCSS and local sample data. No backend or database.

Every product, price, rating and stock level is sample data. Brand names belong to their owners and are used for demonstration only. No real orders are taken and no payments are processed.
